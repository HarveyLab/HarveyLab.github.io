Bio

Fish completed his PhD in the laboratory of Jeff Magee at Baylor College of Medicine, where he studied synaptic, cellular and circuit mechanisms of hippocampal remapping and generalization using in vivo two-photon imaging. In the Harvey lab, Fish is interested in understanding the mechanisms of learning and memory. Outside of lab, Fish loves playing with his cat Blacko, watching UFC, and walking around the city.
